What to look for

The services are Neon, Turso, and PlanetScale. Plans, limits, and pricing can change. Distinguish published facts from fit judgments and do not fill missing prices with estimates.

A successful report contains every planned agent, cited child findings, and a cited master result. A partial report preserves useful sibling results and labels failed agents or missing sources. Research completion and payment confirmation are separate: run reconciliation until authorizations are confirmed or expire unused.
